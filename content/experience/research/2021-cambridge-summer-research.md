**Noise Effects on Low-Resolution Face Recognition Algorithms**

- Implemented Identity-Aware LRFR on synthetic and real low-resolution images.
- Compared recognition accuracy across synthetic and real inputs.
- Demonstrated the performance gap between idealized and real environments.
- Evaluated how Gaussian, Poisson, and pepper-and-salt noise affect recognition performance.
