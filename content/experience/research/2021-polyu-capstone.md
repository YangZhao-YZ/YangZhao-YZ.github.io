**Low-Resolution Face Recognition Algorithms and Noise Effects**

- Implemented face detection and alignment with Multi-task Cascaded Convolutional Networks on FERET and SCface.
- Compared Identity-Aware LRFR with SphereFace for low-resolution face recognition.
- Proposed improvements to make LRFR methods more robust to noise in surveillance-style settings.
