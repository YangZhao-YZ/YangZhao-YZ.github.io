**Synergistic Perception and Control Simplex for Verifiable Safe Vertical Landing**

- Proposed a verifiable solution for safely landing VTOL vehicles in cluttered environments with robust collision-avoidance guarantees.
- Introduced inter-component interactions that reduced landing time while maintaining strong safety guarantees.

**An Optimization-Based Planner with B-Spline Parameterized Continuous-Time Reference Signals**

- Introduced BSPOP to generate continuous-time reference signals that can be tracked by lower-level controllers running at higher frequencies.
- Validated that BSPOP produces better planning paths than same-frequency baseline planners in both simulation and real-world implementation.
