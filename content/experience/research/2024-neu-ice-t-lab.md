**CAffNet: Hard Constraint-Affine Neural Networks**

- Proposed CAffNet, a closed-form neural network framework that guarantees hard satisfaction of an arbitrary number of input-dependent affine inequality constraints without requiring full row rank assumptions.
- Proposed a constraint decomposition with a trainable projection module to handle linearly dependent constraints and non-unique projections.
- Evaluated CAffNet with feedforward and transformer architectures, achieving zero constraint violations with strong task performance.

**Learning Safe-by-Design Neural Network Controllers**

- Proposed a safe-by-design neural network control framework that integrates trainable control barrier functions with CAffNet for guaranteed constraint satisfaction by construction.
- Introduced a more efficient CAffNet formulation that significantly reduces the projection-step cost.
- Demonstrated improved safety enforcement and control performance through extensive simulations.
