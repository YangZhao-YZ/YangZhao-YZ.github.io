## Overview

This independent project studied locomotion for a two-joint snake robot in simulation. The project evaluated how ground-friction modeling affects motion and explored reinforcement-learning control for directional locomotion.

## Motivation

Snake-like robots can move through constrained or irregular environments, but their locomotion depends strongly on contact and friction. Simulation provides a way to test control strategies and friction assumptions before physical deployment.

## System

- Built a two-joint snake robot simulation in MuJoCo.
- Modeled contact between the robot body and the ground.
- Compared isotropic and anisotropic friction effects.

## Control Approach

- Developed reinforcement-learning controllers for directional motion on flat ground.
- Evaluated whether learned policies could generate useful locomotion behaviors under different friction assumptions.
