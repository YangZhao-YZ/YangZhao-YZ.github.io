## Overview

This project developed and tested time-series forecasting models for automated-store customer demand under changing weather, traffic, and time conditions. The work was completed remotely as a Software Engineer at Edison.ai.

## Motivation

Automated-store operations require demand forecasts that can adapt to environmental and temporal factors. This project explored several forecasting approaches and demonstrated their use on structured time-series data.

## Methods

- Tested Vector Autoregression, Long Short-Term Memory networks, and Facebook Prophet.
- Demonstrated the models on an air-pollution forecasting dataset.
- Annotated images with head, hand, and position labels using makesense.ai to support YOLO-based human-detection models.
