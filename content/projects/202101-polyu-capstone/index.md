## Overview

This capstone project investigated low-resolution face recognition algorithms and their robustness to noise. The work was completed at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, advised by Prof. Kenneth K. M. Lam.

## Motivation

Face recognition systems in real-world surveillance settings often operate on low-resolution and noisy face images. This project focused on evaluating recognition pipelines under conditions closer to practical deployment.

## Methods

- Implemented face detection and alignment with Multi-task Cascaded Convolutional Networks.
- Evaluated recognition performance on FERET and SCface.
- Compared Identity-Aware Low-Resolution Face Recognition with SphereFace.
- Studied improvements for making low-resolution recognition methods more robust to noise.
