## Overview

This project studied the robustness of low-resolution face recognition algorithms under realistic image degradation. The work was completed as part of the Online Summer Research Programme at the University of Cambridge, advised by Prof. Marwa Mahmoud.

## Motivation

Low-resolution face recognition is important for surveillance-style settings where face crops are often small, noisy, and captured under non-ideal conditions. A key question is how recognition methods behave when the input differs from idealized synthetic low-resolution images.

## Methods

- Implemented Identity-Aware Low-Resolution Face Recognition on synthetic and real low-resolution images.
- Compared recognition accuracy across synthetic and real image inputs.
- Evaluated the effect of Gaussian, Poisson, and pepper-and-salt noise on recognition performance.
